All-on-4 Implants

A full arch of fixed teeth supported by four angled implants. Many patients leave with a working bridge on the day of surgery, without bone grafting.

1 dayTeeth in a day
4Implants per arch
25+ yrsTypical lifespan
  1. 3D scan and surgical plan
  2. Implant placement under sedation
  3. Same day fixed bridge
  4. Final porcelain bridge at 4 months
Is it painful?

Most patients describe pressure and mild soreness for a few days. Sedation keeps the procedure comfortable.

Who is it suitable for?

It suits people missing most or all teeth in an arch. A scan at your consultation confirms if there is enough bone.

Dental Implants

A titanium post replaces the root, a small connector carries a custom porcelain crown. It looks, feels and cleans like a natural tooth.

3 to 6 moTypical journey
98%Demo success rate
20+ yrsTypical lifespan
  1. Scan and plan the position
  2. Place the implant post
  3. Heal while bone fuses to the post
  4. Fit the abutment and crown
How long is healing?

Bone usually fuses with the post in 8 to 12 weeks. We place a temporary tooth if the gap is visible.

Can I eat with them in?

No. Take them out to eat and drink anything except water, then brush before putting them back.
